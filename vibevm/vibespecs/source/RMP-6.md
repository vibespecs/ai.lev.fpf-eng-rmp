## RMP.6:5 - Archetypal Grounding {#archetypal-grounding}

The following cases have constructed premises. They illustrate choices and returns, not performed studies or actual permissions.

### RMP.6:5.1 - From a reproduced model to a relevant field comparison {#from-a-reproduced-model-to-a-relevant-field-comparison}

The Pump-P4 model produces the reported accelerated-degradation curve from its identified data and code. RMP.5 has retained that computational result and left field relevance unresolved. The question now is whether another rerun would support a longer maintenance interval.

The live threat concerns the mechanism-to-field relation. The existing reproduction already answers its computational question; repeating it cannot supply the missing field comparison. Return the limited model result. If no worthwhile relevant examination is obtainable, finish with the field-use uncertainty intact.

Suppose an independent qualified field dataset then becomes accessible within the available window and can distinguish the competing degradation mechanisms. Select a use-specific validation or mechanism comparison under the supplied domain criteria. Specify the correspondence with the intended field use and the result that would narrow or defeat that use. The decision selects research; only the later actual comparison supplies its result, and the maintenance authority still chooses the interval.

### RMP.6:5.2 - A blocked reproduction is not a contrary result {#a-blocked-reproduction-is-not-a-contrary-result}

An analyst receives a published model claim and code, but the data needed to execute its reported analysis are not accessible under the available rights. The analyst cannot obtain the target computation.

The result is an access-limited attempt, not a reproduction with a discrepant output and not evidence that the underlying claim is false. State the unavailable comparison and what the supplied code or description can still support. If a permitted data arrangement later becomes available, assess and perform the actual reproduction under its criterion.

If that later computation returns a different value, preserve the distinction: now there is an observed discrepancy to examine. Check the target, inputs, environment and justified tolerance before deciding which part of the reported claim changes.

### RMP.6:5.3 - Historical corroboration and a qualitative receiving change {#historical-corroboration-and-a-qualitative-receiving-change}

A directive's date is established, but whether the practice preceded it remains unresolved because an earlier archive series is inaccessible. Reprinting the directive or locating three copies of the same account supplies no independent earlier chronology. The current answer retains that limitation; no further search is automatically commissioned.

An authenticated earlier ledger becomes accessible and can settle the relevant chronology. Source-critical corroboration is now a useful candidate. Its result may defeat the claim that practice began after the directive without establishing the cause of the earlier change.

In a different inquiry, a receiver asks whether a reflexive thematic account proves that most workers share the theme. The live issue is the new prevalence claim. More coder agreement on the same interviews does not supply its population basis. Preserve the situated interpretation and return the new question for an appropriate design. If the actual concern is instead whether the interpretation suppresses a discordant account, examination of that material under the selected qualitative Method can be the relevant contribution.

### RMP.6:5.4 - Two useful examinations and ten days {#two-useful-examinations-and-ten-days}

A research team has ten available investigator-days. Two proposed credibility examinations each need four to six days of non-overlapping effort from that same team. Supplied bounds do not exclude six plus six.

Do not select the pair as an obtainable ten-day programme by separately comparing six plus four and four plus six. Determine whether one examination can answer the current credibility question, whether the arrangement can change, or whether the present answer should retain uncertainty without the pair.

A provider then supplies a qualified shared-preparation arrangement that bounds the combined effort to eight days while preserving both discriminating comparisons. The feasibility answer changes. Reassess the contribution, remaining limits and displaced work before selecting the now obtainable programme; eight days is not itself evidence that the tests are worth doing.

### RMP.6:5.5 - Two different merits answers, one authority boundary {#two-different-merits-answers-one-authority-boundary}

A research protocol requires an additional independent check of a consequential record transformation. Its marginal protection is questioned.

In the first constructed case, supplied examination records show that the check catches an otherwise missed transformation error capable of changing a safety-relevant exposure classification. The baseline controls miss that error, and the check fits the available window without displacing more protective work. The evidence supports retaining that protection or an alternative that preserves its function.

In the second case, supplied records show that the proposed check repeats the same source and procedure, adds no demonstrated discrimination of the protected failure, and displaces a different examination that can expose a consequential error. The merits comparison supports revising or replacing the duplicate requirement under those premises.

These are different substantive recommendations. Neither case transfers amendment authority to the researcher. Retain the in-force condition while returning the evidence, uncertainty, burden and protected-function comparison to its competent owner. A favorable resource argument alone never grants a waiver.

## RMP.6:6 - Bias-Annotation {#bias-annotation}

| Recurring bias | Likely drift | Corrective move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repetition bias | Any repeat is treated as a stronger test of the substantive claim. | Name the precise relation being challenged and the result that could change it. |
| Favorable-outcome bias | Criteria or tested variants are selected after seeing agreement. | Preserve the intended criterion, justified changes and unexamined range. |
| Quantity-of-methods bias | More Methods or sources are treated as independent support. | Recover their distinct contributions, dependence and disagreement logic. |
| Acquisition bias | Every remaining uncertainty becomes another study. | Compare attainable contribution with the full arrangement and allow a bounded completion. |
| Authority shortcut | A requirement is defended by its label or bypassed because it is costly. | Examine protection merits separately from the competent decision and current force. |

## RMP.6:8 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them {#common-anti-patterns-and-how-to-avoid-them}

| Anti-pattern | Why it fails | Practical correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “The rerun validates the model” | The computation can agree while the intended-use relation remains unsupported. | Select the examination of that relation under supplied criteria. |
| “The replication was not significant, so the effect is absent” | The label omits magnitude, uncertainty and the chosen comparison. | Interpret the actual result under its design and criterion. |
| “Many copies corroborate the source” | Repeated carriers can preserve one dependent account. | Examine origin, dependence and the distinct evidential contribution. |
| “No data access means the claim failed” | An unattemptable comparison supplies no contrary output. | Return the access limit and the exact examination still unavailable. |
| “Each test fits, so the programme fits” | Shared demands can exceed the total available arrangement. | Check admitted joint combinations and choose a feasible useful set. |
| “We selected the test, so credibility increased” | A prospective decision supplies no performed result. | Keep the claim's current support unchanged until actual evidence warrants revision. |
| “It is required, therefore it protects” | Current force does not establish incremental protection. | Examine merits and return amendment to its responsible authority. |

## RMP.6:10 - Rationale {#rationale}

A credibility test is informative through the relation between its possible result and the claim. That relation explains both why one well-targeted comparison can matter and why a battery of mismatched tests can add little.

Evidence adequacy and acquisition choice are different questions. Preserving both allows a researcher to finish the present allocation honestly while keeping uncertainty visible. Separating prospective decisions from actual results prevents an approved study plan from being mistaken for support.

Protective requirements also make distinct claims about value, causal or measurement effects, practical burden and authority. Examining one does not automatically establish or cancel the others.

## RMP.6:11 - SoTA-Echoing {#sota-echoing}

The working question is which examination can improve or revise confidence in a bounded research claim, under what criterion, and at what obtainable burden.

The [National Academies' 2019 synthesis](https://doi.org/10.17226/25303) supplies the useful distinction between computational reproduction and new-data replication. It remains an anchor rather than a universal ladder.

The 2026 SCORE accounts refine different questions in social and behavioural research. The [reproducibility study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10203-5) distinguishes availability of usable data from computational reproduction. The [replicability study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10078-y) makes differing success criteria consequential. The [analytical-robustness study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09844-9) examines defensible analytical alternatives. RMP.6 retains those distinctions without importing the studies' sampled fields, rates or tolerances as universal criteria.

[Braun and Clarke's qualitative guidance](https://www.thematicanalysis.net/editor-checklist/) and [2024 RTARG account](https://doi.org/10.1177/02692163241234800) preserve methodological coherence for reflexive thematic analysis. The [American Historical Association's standards](https://www.historians.org/resource/statement-on-standards-of-professional-conduct/) retain source integrity and criticism for historical claims. Neither implies that all credibility questions should use the same repetition or agreement procedure.

The [2021 MRC framework](https://www.bmj.com/content/374/bmj.n2061) supports question-dependent perspectives, context and research's resource consequences in complex-intervention research. RMP.6 carries that contrast into the local choice without imposing intervention phases on every domain.

These sources do not supply the actual data, domain threshold, empirical result or authority. Reopen the affected comparison when a stronger methodological account, changed source, criterion, obtainable arrangement or receiving use changes what the test could establish.
